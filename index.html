💭 Key Words (Normal in Puberty)

• Sexual Fantasy: Imagination in mind

• Sexual Impulse: Strong desire from a trigger

A. Set limits of intimacy — Say "Stop!"

B. Stay away from pornography — No 18+ videos

C. Avoid quiet places — Stay in public places

D. Join healthy activities — Play sports / cycle
